why a burrow?

I like rabbits. Rabbits have burrows. Naturally, I’ll have my own burrow. Duh.

This is essentially a space where I dump all my bullshit for public access. Most of this exists because I got frustrated that nothing was detailed enough for what I actually wanted to write, so I built it from scratch. Severely poor impulse control and overly ambitious ideas have been a consistent contributor to this page’s existence.

I do this for the love of the game. I want something, I make it. If it doesn’t fit my needs, I make something else.

If something here is useful to you, take it. If you want to see something specific, I'm always open to suggestions — just know my turnaround time is when inspiration strikes, which is a polite way of saying when I feel like it.
